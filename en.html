About the Doctor

Advanced Medical Expertise

Dr. Ahmed AlMohammed is a distinguished medical professional in the fields of dermatology and non-surgical aesthetic medicine. His career combines a strong academic foundation—holding both the Saudi and Arab Boards—with deep clinical experience gained through practice in leading government hospitals and specialized centers.

Dr. Ahmed's philosophy is rooted in the belief that aesthetics is not about erasing features, but rather an art and science aimed at restoring radiance and enhancing natural beauty in a safe and thoughtful manner. He is known for his meticulous approach to evaluating each patient and designing customized treatment plans based on the latest globally proven techniques to ensure outstanding and sustainable results.

Education & Credentials

  • Certified by the Saudi Board of Dermatology (2020).
  • Certified by the Arab Board of Dermatology (2020).
  • Bachelor of Medicine & Surgery (2014).

Leadership & Experience

  • Chief Resident in the Dermatology Program.
  • Consultant at leading government hospitals.
  • Expert in injectable aesthetics and laser procedures.

Scientific & Research Contributions

Beyond clinical practice, Dr. Ahmed has actively contributed to scientific research, with studies exploring community behaviors toward aesthetic medicine. His most notable publications include:

  • A study on the sources from which the community receives medical information about dermatology and aesthetics.
  • A detailed investigation into the impact of image editing on social media on the decision to seek aesthetic services.
