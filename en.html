Meet the Doctor

Advanced Medical Expertise

Dr. Ahmed AlMohammed is one of the distinguished medical professionals in the field of dermatology and non-surgical cosmetic medicine. His career combines a solid academic foundation—holding both the Saudi and Arab Board certifications—with deep practical experience gained through his medical practice in major government hospitals and specialized centers.

Dr. Ahmed's philosophy is rooted in a profound belief that cosmetic treatment is not about erasing features, but rather an art and science aimed at restoring radiance and highlighting natural beauty in a safe and thoughtful manner. He is known for his meticulous approach in evaluating each patient's condition individually and designing customized treatment plans based on the latest globally proven technologies to ensure remarkable and lasting results.

Academic Qualifications

  • Holder of the Saudi Board in Dermatology (2020).
  • Holder of the Arab Board in Dermatology (2020).
  • Bachelor of Medicine and Surgery (2014).

Leadership Experience

  • Chief Resident in the Dermatology Residency Program.
  • Consultant at leading government hospitals.
  • Expert in cosmetic injections and laser treatments.

Scientific & Research Contributions

Dr. Ahmed has not limited himself to clinical practice; he has actively contributed to enriching scientific research through studies that highlight community behaviors toward cosmetic medicine. Among his most notable published research:

  • A study on the sources from which the community receives medical information about dermatology and cosmetics.
  • In-depth research on the impact of photo editing on social media on decisions to seek cosmetic services.
